Ventoy on Drives Over 2TB with MBR Partitioning

This article examines how the multi-boot tool Ventoy operates when installed on storage drives larger than 2TB using the Master Boot Record (MBR) partition scheme. Due to the inherent 32-bit logical block addressing limitations of MBR, drives exceeding 2TB cannot have their full capacity addressed under standard sector sizes. While Ventoy permits MBR installation on these high-capacity drives, it confines its partition boundaries within the 2TB limit, leaving the remaining storage unusable unless specific sector configurations or the GPT partition style are utilized.

The 2TB MBR Architectural Limit

Standard MBR partitioning relies on 32-bit values to store sector addresses. On modern storage drives using standard 512-byte logical sector emulation (512e), the maximum addressable storage capacity is 2,199,023,255,552 bytes (approximately 2.2TB or 2TiB). Any storage beyond this boundary cannot be indexed, partitioned, or read by an MBR partition table.

Ventoy's Partition Structure

When installed, Ventoy splits a storage drive into two distinct partitions:

  1. Partition 1 (ExFAT/NTFS/ext4): The primary data partition where ISOs, images, and user files are stored.
  2. Partition 2 (FAT): A small, hidden EFI system partition labeled VTOYEFI containing the bootloaders and core files required to launch the Ventoy environment in both legacy BIOS and UEFI modes.

How Ventoy Behaves with MBR on Drives Larger Than 2TB

When you instruct the Ventoy installer to format a drive larger than 2TB using MBR:

  • Truncation of Usable Space: Ventoy creates its two partitions strictly within the first 2TB of the disk. The main image partition occupies the majority of this addressable space, followed by the hidden VTOYEFI partition placed at or near the 2TB boundary.
  • Unallocated Remainder: All storage capacity exceeding the 2TB threshold is left unallocated. Operating systems cannot create secondary partitions or extend the existing Ventoy partition into this area under an MBR layout.
  • Boot Functionality: The drive remains functional for booting. Both legacy BIOS and UEFI systems can boot from the drive, provided the machine's firmware can read the primary MBR partition table.

The Exception: 4Kn Drives

If the target drive uses native 4K sectors (4096 bytes per sector without 512e emulation), MBR can technically address up to 16TB. In this rare scenario, Ventoy using MBR can utilize the full capacity of a drive up to 16TB. However, most external hard drives and USB flash drives expose 512-byte logical sectors to maintain backward compatibility, subjecting them to the standard 2TB ceiling.

Recommended Alternative: Switching to GPT

To access the full capacity of a drive larger than 2TB, configure Ventoy to use GUID Partition Table (GPT) instead of MBR:

  1. Open the Ventoy installation tool.
  2. Navigate to Option > Partition Style.
  3. Select GPT before installing Ventoy to the target drive.

GPT removes the 2TB barrier, allowing the primary Ventoy data partition to expand across the entire storage volume. Modern UEFI systems boot natively from GPT drives without issue. However, selecting GPT may limit bootability on older legacy BIOS-only machines that lack hybrid or protective MBR support.
