Ventoy vs iVentoy Licensing Differences

While both Ventoy and iVentoy share a common developer and core goal of simplifying system deployments, their licensing frameworks are fundamentally different. Standard Ventoy is a traditional, fully free and open-source utility intended for direct USB media creation, whereas iVentoy is a proprietary, network-based PXE boot solution that operates on a freemium and commercial licensing model. Understanding these differences is critical for organizations and individuals ensuring software compliance during system deployments.

Ventoy: Free and Open-Source (GPLv3)

Standard Ventoy is distributed under the GNU General Public License version 3.0 (GPLv3). Under this license:

  • Source Code Access: The complete source code is publicly accessible, allowing users to inspect, modify, and build the software independently.
  • Commercial Freedom: You can use standard Ventoy within personal, educational, non-profit, or commercial enterprise environments completely free of charge.
  • Redistribution Terms: You may redistribute the software or modified versions of it, provided that any derivative work is also released under the terms of the GPLv3 license.
  • Feature Limits: There are no artificial software restrictions, device caps, or feature paywalls in standard Ventoy.

iVentoy: Proprietary Multi-Tier Licensing

Unlike standard Ventoy, iVentoy is not an open-source project. It uses closed-source backend software for PXE network booting and operates under a dual-tier proprietary license divided into a Free edition and a Commercial (Pro) edition.

1. iVentoy Free Edition

The Free edition of iVentoy is designed for home users, testing, and evaluation:

  • Non-Commercial Use: It is free to use for personal, educational, and testing purposes.
  • Client Device Limits: It includes built-in operational constraints, specifically limiting the number of concurrent client machines that can boot via the network at the same time (capped at 20 concurrent clients).
  • Proprietary Code: The source code is closed, meaning users cannot audit, modify, or recompile the software.

2. iVentoy Pro (Commercial Edition)

The Pro edition is aimed at business, IT support, and enterprise environments:

  • Commercial Rights: Organizations and businesses must purchase a Pro license to legally deploy iVentoy in a commercial environment or as part of commercial IT services.
  • Unlimited Deployment: The Pro edition removes the concurrent client restriction, allowing an unlimited number of systems to boot simultaneously over the local network.
  • Licensing Model: Licenses are sold as lifetime activations with specified upgrade windows, tied to specific hardware configurations or site usage depending on the selected commercial tier.

Summary of Differences

  • Source Code: Ventoy is fully open-source (GPLv3); iVentoy is proprietary and closed-source.
  • Commercial Cost: Ventoy is completely free for business deployment; iVentoy requires a paid commercial license for business use.
  • Functional Restrictions: Ventoy has zero functional caps; iVentoy restricts concurrent clients unless upgraded to the paid tier.
