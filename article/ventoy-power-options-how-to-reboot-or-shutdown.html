Ventoy Power Options: How to Reboot or Shutdown

Ventoy provides built-in power management controls directly within its bootloader interface, allowing users to safely reboot, power down, or access firmware settings without relying on physical hardware buttons. This guide explains how to navigate Ventoy's power options, the hotkeys used to trigger reboot and shutdown functions, and how these capabilities streamline system administration and troubleshooting workflows.

Accessing Power Controls in the Ventoy Menu

When your computer boots into the Ventoy USB drive, power management features are integrated directly into the primary graphical interface. You do not need to boot into an operating system or perform a hard reset to turn off or restart the computer.

There are two primary ways to manage power in Ventoy:

  1. The F5 Tools Menu: Pressing the F5 key on your keyboard opens the internal Ventoy Tools menu. Within this secondary menu, navigate using the arrow keys to find options labeled Power, Reboot, or Halt (Shutdown). Selecting the desired action and pressing Enter executes the command immediately.

  2. Dedicated Hotkeys: Depending on the installed Ventoy version and the active theme, direct hotkeys are often mapped on the main screen. Looking at the bottom hotkey bar of the interface, you will typically find:

    • Reboot: Often mapped to Ctrl + r or dedicated menu entries to initiate an immediate, clean system restart.
    • Power Off / Halt: Often mapped to Ctrl + h, Ctrl + s, or accessible via the function key menu to safely halt the system and cut power.

Rebooting into UEFI Firmware Settings

Modern systems running Ventoy in UEFI mode often include an advanced power feature: the ability to reboot directly into the motherboard's UEFI/BIOS configuration screen.

Inside the F5 Tools menu, look for an entry named UEFI Firmware Settings (or similar). Selecting this instructs the motherboard's EFI boot manager to launch the hardware setup utility upon the subsequent restart, eliminating the need to rapidly press setup keys (such as Del or F2) during the machine's initial POST sequence.

Benefits of In-Menu Power Management

  • Hardware Protection: Hard-powering off a machine using the physical power switch or pulling the plug can cause electrical spikes or damage file systems on attached drives. Using Ventoy's software halt ensures the motherboard conducts an orderly ACPI shutdown.
  • Convenient Media Swapping: If you inserted the wrong external drive or need to adjust BIOS boot priorities, rebooting directly from the menu saves time.
  • Automated & Custom Menus: Through the ventoy.json configuration file, advanced users can customize themes, alter hotkey behaviors, or hide specific power entries to prevent unauthorized restarts in kiosk or technician environments.
